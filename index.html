Frequently asked questions

Can I use Chalkwise on my iPad and my iPhone?

Yes, with the same Apple Account, and Pro works on both. Chalkwise does not sync between devices: your classes live on the device where you enter them. To move them, save a backup file in Settings > Backup & restore and restore it on the other device.

I have a new iPad. How do I keep my classes?

On the old device: Settings > Backup & restore > Save a backup file (for example to iCloud Drive). On the new device: install Chalkwise, tap "Moving from another device? Restore a backup file" on the first screen (or Settings > Backup & restore > Restore from a backup file) and pick the file. If you bought Pro, also tap Settings > Unlock Pro > Restore purchases, so that all your classes stay editable.

I made a mistake. Can I go back?

Changes to grades, attendance and seating, and deletions, can be undone with Undo until you close the app. Chalkwise also keeps a copy for each day you use it, the last 14, on the device (Settings > Backup & restore).

What is free?

2 active classes with the full gradebook, your own grading scales, terms, attendance, seating, timetable and every tool. Archived classes and the sample class do not count; without Pro, archived classes are read-only. CSV export is free. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited classes and PDF reports, with Family Sharing.

Does my school allow this?

Student data stays on your device, apart from files you share and your device's own iCloud or Google backup if it is on. Your school decides what student information you may keep on a personal device; the privacy policy has a section for schools.

I am leaving my school. How do I delete the class data?

Settings > Delete all data removes every class and the automatic copies from the device; deleting the app does the same. Backup files and reports you saved or shared elsewhere stay where you put them, so delete those there too. Your Pro purchase is kept with your Apple Account.
