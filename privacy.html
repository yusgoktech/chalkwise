Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud server.
  • No ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • Your classes, students, grades, attendance and notes stay on your device. We never receive them.

Data you enter

Chalkwise stores the class data you enter (class names, student names and numbers, grades, attendance, notes, seating charts, timetable and lesson notes) in a file inside the app on your device. It is included in your device's own iCloud or Google backup if you have turned that on. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Chalkwise keeps automatic copies of this file on your device so you can recover from mistakes: one for each day you use the app, the last 14 of them, and up to 3 copies made before a backup is restored. Backup files, PDF reports and CSV files are created on your device and go only where you choose to save or send them.

You, or your school, decide what student data you enter and remain responsible for it. Please follow your school's rules on keeping student information on a personal device.

For schools and data protection officers

  • Chalkwise has no server and no user accounts. We cannot see, access or recover any class or student data.
  • Data on the device is protected by the device’s own encryption and, if the teacher turns it on, by the app lock (Face ID, Touch ID or passcode).
  • Backup files, PDF reports and CSV files are not encrypted by the app. Store them like any other student record.
  • Deleting a class or the app removes its data from the device. A deleted class can remain in the automatic copies on the device until they are replaced (the last 14 days of use); deleting the app removes them too.
  • The teacher or the school decides on and is responsible for the student data. We never receive it, so we do not process it on anyone’s behalf.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on the app lock, the operating system checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Chalkwise only receives a yes or no answer and never has access to biometric data.

Purchases

The optional one-time Chalkwise Pro purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

To verify purchases and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase history (product, date, store receipt) and basic device and app information needed for verification. No class or student data is ever sent. We do not link this data to your name or e-mail, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. We only see overall sales figures (for example, the number of purchases per country) in RevenueCat and App Store Connect. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Ratings

The app may ask the operating system to show its standard rating prompt. Apple or Google decide whether it is shown, and any rating you give is handled by them.

Children

Chalkwise is a tool for teachers and is not directed at children. Student information entered by a teacher stays on the teacher's device and is not collected by us.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you or your students, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete class data in the app at any time; Settings > Delete all data removes everything, including the automatic copies. For purchase records you can contact Apple or Google. You can also ask us to request deletion of the anonymous RevenueCat record of your app installation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
